FM Interface firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1.

File: fm-interface.fwsc
SHA-256: 6ee804b7dfe07376e5f4ed8af504a84ebdd546c16f84c7b0abcec689d9cc1264

This image has been linked and checked on a host. It has not been flashed to a device.
Spectrum together with aptX and full USB recording does not all fit at once; if a
graph cannot allocate, the screen falls back to meters. Audio and the sequencer still run.

Install with M-Flasher (https://github.com/jvitkauskas/m-flasher):
1. Use a data USB cable. Do not unplug or power off during the write.
2. If the FM-1 is still on stock M-VAVE firmware, install m-bootloader first.
   Audio Router, Melodee, Felucca, and other fm1-base firmwares already have it.
3. Select fm-interface.fwsc.
4. Keep bootloader replacement OFF. Do not erase the whole chip.
5. Leave "Also erase settings and Bluetooth pairings" unchecked.
6. If M-Flasher cannot see the running firmware, power on while holding REC, then flash.

After it boots, USB audio is "FM1 Audio". SEQ opens NOTES, the piano roll. Press SEQ
again for STEPS, PATTERN, then TEMPO. PATTERN sets 1, 2 or 4 bars. Stopped, on NOTES
or STEPS, a key or chord writes the cursor step and releasing the keys moves on.
ARP opens the arpeggiator. PLAY/STOP starts and stops MIDI clock and the sequence,
and does not mute incoming audio. If the master was muted by the previous firmware,
press PLAY once and the audio comes back. REC on a musical page captures MIDI and
leaves the incoming audio running. A free take is played on PATTERN, TEMPO or ARP.
